## Introduction
This guide explains how to manage user roles and permissions within the application. Understanding these concepts will help you control access to different features and resources.

## Available Roles

### System Roles
1. **App Owner**
   - Full access to all features and settings
   - Can manage all users and projects
   - Can assign any role to any user

2. **Admin**
   - Almost full system access
   - Can manage most resources and users
   - Cannot modify App Owner accounts
   - Cannot assign admin role to other users

### Project Roles
1. **Project Owner**
   - Full control over a specific project
   - Can add/remove team members
   - Can modify project settings and content

2. **Team Member**
   - Can view and contribute to project content
   - Limited access to project settings
   - Cannot manage team members

## Managing Team Members

### Managing Team Members
1. Navigate to the project
2. Click on the "Team" tab to view all team members and their roles
3. Only project owners and administrators can modify team membership

Note: The team member role is binary - a user is either a team member or not. Additional functional roles (like Designer, Engineer, etc.) are managed separately and can be viewed in the team list.

### Removing a Team Member
1. Go to the project's "Team" tab
2. Find the team member
3. Click the "Remove" button
4. Confirm the removal

## Common Tasks

### Requesting Access
If you need access to a project or feature:
1. Contact your project owner or system administrator
2. Provide your email address and the required access level

### Reporting Access Issues
If you can't access a resource you believe you should have access to:
1. Check if you're logged in with the correct account
2. Verify your role in the project
3. Contact your project owner or system administrator

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### "Access Denied" Message
- You don't have the required permissions
- Your session might have expired
- Try logging out and back in

#### Missing Features
- Your role might not have access to certain features
- Check with your project owner about upgrading your role

#### Can't Add Team Members
- Only Project Owners and Administrators can manage team members
- Verify you have the correct role in the project
- Contact your project owner or system administrator for assistance

### Getting Help
For additional assistance, contact your system administrator or email support@example.com.
